Periodization

Three single-file training apps. Each one keeps its own log on this device — open one, then add it to your home screen.

PPL Block

Push / Pull / Legs / Full Body on five-week blocks. Asks the RP soreness, pump and volume questions and moves your sets from the answers.

Rolling Five

A five-slot rolling cycle — push, pull, legs, and two skill days. Double progression on the bar, position progression on the handstand pushup and front lever.

Prove It

Upper / lower, four days a week, for building muscle. Every exercise ends in a proof set taken to failure — and the app checks what you say about it against what you actually lifted.

Both run offline once loaded and store everything locally. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
